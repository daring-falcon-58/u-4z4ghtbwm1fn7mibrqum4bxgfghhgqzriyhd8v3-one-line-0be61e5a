## Winner

**#1 — "My therapist told me to face my fears, so I opened my banking app." (66 chars, 24/25)**

Why it wins, criterion by criterion:
- **Laugh / turn:** setup ("face my fears") primes a brave, therapeutic act; the
  punchline reframes the scariest thing in the speaker's life as an ordinary
  banking app. The twist lands in the last four words — a real turn, not an
  observation.
- **Tightness:** 15 words, nothing trimmable; removing any word breaks the setup
  or the punch.
- **Freshness:** the "bank balance as fear" angle is a lived, current anxiety,
  not a recycled pun; the phrasing is original, not a known joke.
- **Clarity:** one read, no explanation, no second line needed.
- **Tie-break edge:** at 66 bytes it is shorter than most entries, and the
  competition breaks exact win-rate ties toward the smaller text, then the
  earlier receipt.
